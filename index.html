    Which ideas can go LIVENEW
    
      
    Switched on = a card from that source can be approved and sent to Robinhood. Switched off = a PAPER card: shown, scored, never sent. Turning one off works right away; turning one on starts the next trading day at 9:30 AM.
    
      
      
      
    
    
    Kill switchNEW
    
      
    When a limit below is hit: no new cards until it resets. Signals keep listening; ideas become paper cards and are scored. When off, those limits only add a red flag to cards.
